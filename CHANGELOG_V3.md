# V3 Change Log

## Built from lessons in the reference credit study
- Preserves the study's four qualitative pillars and financial score concept.
- Adds working-capital need calculation instead of treating requested amount as self-justifying.
- Adds pro-forma leverage and cash-flow repayment gates.
- Separates primary repayment source from collateral as secondary mitigation.
- Adds legal enforceability and collateral valuation freshness.
- Adds missing-information penalties for AR aging, inventory aging, insurance and bureau checks.
- Adds customer/supplier concentration and FX/import risk.
- Adds automatic credit-policy exceptions.
- Adds multi-factor stress testing.
- Adds indicative IFRS 9 Stage / PD / LGD / EAD / ECL module.
