# V4 Model & Governance Card

## Purpose
Decision support for corporate credit underwriting.

## Human accountability
The system does not have approval authority. Extracted values must be reviewed by an analyst, and final approval must follow the institution's delegated authorities and policies.

## Extraction
V4 uses deterministic parsing and label matching. Every extracted value carries a source location and confidence indicator. Low-confidence or missing fields must be reviewed.

## Credit engine
The engine combines financial capacity, qualitative inputs, data quality, stress testing, working-capital need, leverage constraints, and collateral controls.

## IFRS 9
Any PD/LGD/EAD/ECL output is indicative until calibrated on institution-specific data, independently validated, governed, and aligned with applicable accounting and supervisory requirements.

## Confidentiality
No confidential reference case is identified, quoted, or embedded in this repository.
